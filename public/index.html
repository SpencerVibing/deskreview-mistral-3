Privacy first - your manuscript stays yours. How it works

DeskReview uses Mistral to process your manuscript only for the review you request. Our Mistral API account is configured to opt out of model training, so your uploaded content is not used to train Mistral's models.

Key features

Every check you need, tied back to the manuscript.

DeskReview keeps checks compact on the surface and source-grounded underneath, so every finding can be opened, verified, and reused in feedback.

Integrity and completeness checks

Counts, source-reference checks, and jump links.

DeskReview checks core manuscript elements such as authors, affiliations, abstract, article text, keywords, references, tables, and figures. Details open into source-linked panes so users can verify what was counted and where body-text references occur.
